Privacy Policy

GL-SD007 rclone

Purpose

GL-SD007 rclone is a personal research utility used by the project owner to archive and verify research data in the owner's Google Drive.

Google user data accessed

The app requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. This allows the app to create, read, update, and delete only the Google Drive files that the user creates or opens with this app.

How Google user data is used

Google Drive access is used only to upload research archive files, verify archived file contents and integrity, retry failed archive operations, and manage files created by this app as part of the research backup workflow.

Storage

Research archive files are stored in the project owner's Google Drive. rclone OAuth tokens and configuration are stored locally on the owner's computer by rclone. OAuth client secrets and access tokens are not published on this website or intentionally shared with third parties.

Sharing

Google user data accessed by this app is not sold and is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing. The utility is intended for the owner's personal research use.

Retention and deletion

Archive files are retained according to the owner's research retention policy. The owner may delete archived files from Google Drive at any time. Local OAuth authorization can also be revoked by removing the rclone remote or revoking the app's Google Account access.

Revoking access

The user can revoke this application's Google Account access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future Google Drive API operations until authorization is granted again.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's data-access behavior changes. The published version on this site describes the current intended use.
