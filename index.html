GL-SD007 rclone

Personal research archive utility

GL-SD007 rclone is a personal utility used to archive immutable research data from the GL-SD007 project to the project owner's Google Drive.

What the app does

The app uses rclone and the Google Drive API to create, read back, verify, and manage archive files created by this app. It is used only for the owner's research-data backup and integrity-verification workflow.

Google Drive access

The app requests the drive.file permission. This limits access to Google Drive files created or opened by this app rather than granting general access to all files in the user's Drive.

Data handling

Research archive files are stored in the project owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials and rclone configuration are kept locally on the owner's computer. The app does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.
